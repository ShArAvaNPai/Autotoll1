Chapter 9: Conclusion and Future Enhancement (Pages 29-30)

9.1 Conclusion
The AutoToll AI system successfully demonstrates the feasibility of a low-cost, high-efficiency automated toll collection solution. By integrating YOLOv8 for detection and EasyOCR for text recognition, the system achieves a balance between accuracy and computational speed. The hybrid architecture, utilizing both local SQLite caching and cloud-based Supabase synchronization, ensures that the system is both fast for real-time operations and reliable for long-term data storage. The implementation of a digital wallet system directly addresses the objective of reducing congestion at toll plazas by eliminating the need for physical cash handling.

9.2 Future Enhancement
- Edge Computing Optimization: Transitioning the AI inference logic to specialized hardware like NVIDIA Jetson or dedicated TPUs to support higher frame rates and 4K camera streams.
- OCR Precision: Fine-tuning a custom OCR model specifically on Indian license plate fonts (HSRP) to reduce the 6/S and 0/O confusion even further without relying on RegEx post-processing.
- Mobile App Integration: Developing a cross-platform mobile application (Flutter/React Native) for vehicle owners to receive instant push notifications and perform UPI-based wallet top-ups.
- Multi-Lane Scaling: Expanding the backend concurrency model to handle tens of lanes simultaneously using a distributed message queue (RabbitMQ/Kafka).
