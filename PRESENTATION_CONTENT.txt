## Slide 2: The Problem
- **Congestion**: Hours wasted in long queues at manual toll plazas.
- **Human Error**: Manual logging leads to revenue leakage and entry mistakes.
- **Safety**: Close physical contact for payments (post-pandemic relevance).
- **Environmental Impact**: Idling vehicles contribute to excessive fuel consumption and emissions.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution - AutoToll AI
- **Automation**: High-speed vehicle detection and license plate recognition.
- **Digital Payments**: Automatic wallet deduction for contactless travel.
- **Efficiency**: Reduces transaction time from minutes to milliseconds.
- **Scalability**: Centralized database architecture for multi-plaza synchronization.

---

## Slide 4: Technology Stack
- **Frontend**: React.js (Vite) + Tailwind CSS (Modern, Responsive UI).
- **Backend**: Python FastAPI (High-performance API layer).
- **AI Models**: 
  - YOLOv8 for overall vehicle detection.
  - YOLOv9-tiny + MobileViT for specialized license plate OCR.
- **Database**: SQLite (Edge storage) & Supabase (Cloud synchronization).

---

## Slide 5: System Workflow
1. **Detection**: IP Camera captures vehicle movement.
2. **Analysis**: AI crops the plate and reads the alphanumeric text.
3. **Verification**: Backend checks plate against registered database.
4. **Transaction**:
   - **Registered**: Toll deducted from wallet balance.
   - **Unregistered/Low Balance**: System flags for manual review/cash collection.
5. **Logging**: Real-time record creation and cloud sync.

---

## Slide 6: AI & Machine Learning Cycle
- **Collect**: Real-world image gathering from toll cameras.
- **Label**: Annotating plate locations for model training.
- **Train**: Iterative learning using YOLOv8 to recognize diverse plate formats.
- **Active Learning**: System identifies low-confidence captures for future retraining, making the AI smarter over time.
